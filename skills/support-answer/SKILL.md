# Support Answer

Find and read the canonical product, requirements, GTM, and active-initiative pages relevant to the question. Lead with a plain-language answer suitable for the stated audience. Distinguish confirmed current behaviour from drafts, plans, unknowns, or unsupported assumptions.

Never invent a policy, promise a delivery date, or give an explanation that the sources do not support. State the limitation and appropriate escalation owner when the documentation cannot answer safely.
