# Estimation Jam record

Add this section to the canonical initiative document after the initiative’s scope, risks, and dependencies are sufficiently clear. Keep only decision-relevant evidence; link to source material instead of duplicating it.

```markdown
## Estimation Jam

### Readiness

- Outcome and success measure: [confirmed / gap]
- First outcome slice and out-of-scope boundary: [confirmed / gap]
- Systems, integrations, and dependencies: [confirmed / unknowns]
- Main discovery and delivery risks: [confirmed / gap]
- Delivery team and material capacity constraints: [confirmed / gap]

### Evidence used

| Reference initiative or slice | Team | Delivery state | Why comparable / key difference | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Initiative or slice] | [Team] | [Released / production-ready / blocked / stopped] | [Comparison] | [Wiki, GitHub, Linear, or other links] |

### Systems and repository evidence

- [System or integration] — [repository links or other technical evidence] — confirmed by [team member/role]

### Agent recommendation

- Proposed Jam Load: [1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 / split required]
- Confidence: [high / medium / low] — [why]
- Comparison reasoning: [closest anchors and relevant differences]
- Delivery-context adjustment: [recent AI, automation, platform, or capability change; or none]

### Acceleration options considered

| Phase | Suggested method | Prerequisite and quality evidence | Team decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery / implementation / validation / go-live | [Specific AI-assisted or other method] | [What must be true and how quality is checked] | [Accepted / adapted / rejected — why] |

### Team decision

- Agreed Jam Load: [1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 / split required]
- Confidence: [high / medium / low] — [why]
- Assumptions and dependencies: [list]
- Dissent or material challenge: [list, or none]
- Optional suggested next action: [action and owner, or “None — ready for commitment”]
- Participants and date: [people/roles, date]

### Later evidence refresh (optional)

- Actual discovery / implementation / go-live shape: [brief note]
- Material scope changes, blockers, or delivery-context changes: [brief note]
- Calibration note for future Jams: [what this confirms, adjusts, or leaves uncertain]
```

Do not convert Jam Loads into universal days or person-weeks. Cycle planning may compare the team’s completed load across recent cycles, together with dependencies and confidence, to judge whether a portfolio feels sustainable.
